# Evaluation Usability and Compatibility Checklist

Record the browser, operating system, screen width, date, tester, and any
defects observed for every completed session.

## Administrator workflow

- Sign in and open the dashboard.
- Create, edit, search, deactivate, and restore a medicine record.
- Receive a batch and verify expiry, quantity, and FEFO order.
- Dispense or sell enough units to verify stock totals.
- Approve a procurement request and verify its status.
- Download CSV and PDF reports and compare key totals.

## Pharmacist workflow

- Sign in with pharmacist credentials.
- Verify permitted stock and alert actions.
- Confirm that staff-management administration is unavailable.
- Create a transaction and verify the audit trail.
- Open the forecast view and verify 30-, 60-, and 90-day points.

## Compatibility

- Chrome desktop
- Firefox desktop
- Edge desktop
- Mobile viewport
- Keyboard-only navigation
- Zoomed browser view

## Accessibility checks

- Labels are visible and associated with form controls.
- Validation errors are understandable and reachable.
- Keyboard focus is visible.
- Text and controls remain usable at 200 percent zoom.
- Status changes are communicated in the interface.
- Tables and charts have understandable text alternatives.
